About me

Hi! I'm Sebastijan, an 18-year-old Computer Science student from Slovenia. I've always been curious about how technology works and what happens behind the things we use every day.

What started as curiosity gradually turned into a genuine interest in software development. I enjoy taking an idea, breaking it down into smaller problems, and turning it into something that actually works.

How I Learn

I learn best by building things. Instead of only reading about a concept, I like to experiment with it, make mistakes, figure out why something doesn't work, and try again.

Personal projects have become one of the most important parts of my learning process. They give me an opportunity to take what I've learned and apply it to real problems while discovering new things along the way.

What Drives Me

I'm naturally curious and enjoy understanding how things work beneath the surface. Whether it's software, or an operating system, I like going beyond simply knowing how to use something and understanding why it works the way it does.

I also value consistency. I believe that becoming good at something isn't about learning everything at once, but about improving a little every day and staying willing to learn from mistakes.

Looking Ahead

I'm still at the beginning of my journey, and that's something I see as an opportunity rather than a limitation. There is a lot I still want to learn, build, and experience.

My goal is to keep developing both technically and personally, work on increasingly meaningful projects, and eventually contribute to software that solves real-world problems.
